Contact

Get in touch

To bring a workshop to your group, propose a partnership, ask for the methodological material or deal with your data.

Email

Write to contato@ponte-em-cena.com.br. It is the project address and it reaches both organisations behind it.

There is no form. A form would mean sending what you write to a third-party service before it reaches us, and for the volume of messages we get that is not worth it. A direct email puts fewer people in between.

Bringing a workshop to your group

We work with groups that already exist: classes, collectives, cultural programmes, teams, communities, always in partnership with the organisation hosting the group. There is no individual sign-up.

Tell us in the email who the group is, how many people, what age range, and what subject you would want to work through. If you have read the three workshops, say which one sounds closest to what you need.

About your data

To exercise any right under the LGPD or the GDPR (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability) use the same address and say what you are asking for. How that works and what we keep is set out on the privacy page.

Press and social

For press, the same email. On Instagram the project is @ponteemcena.
