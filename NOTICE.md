## Data — attribution is a condition of use

**Lobelia Earth, _Brasil in a +2ºC World_** — an initiative of Lobelia and
Instituto Itaúsa. <https://portal.brasil2c.com/>. Accessed **07/08/2026**.

Powers the municipal map at `/mapa-agua/`. Three obligations travel with it
wherever the map goes, and all three are on that page:

1. the source line with the access date and a link to the portal;
2. the **screening-level caveat** — the data does not replace field assessment,
   environmental impact study or legal opinion;
3. notifying `brasil2c@lobelia.earth` that work using it is public.
   **Still outstanding** — tracked as `dft-lobelia-notify-2026-08-07`.

Two accuracy points that must not be softened: the rainfall trend is *measured*
1980–2015, not projected, and the +2 °C case carries **no calendar date**.

Upstream sources Lobelia credits: JRC Global River Flood Hazard Maps, WRI
Aqueduct Floods, IBGE landslide susceptibility, CHIRPS v3, bias-adjusted CORDEX
projections, MapBiomas Collection 9, ESA CCI Burned Area.

## Derived work

`/mapa-agua/` is generated by `tools/make-water-map.py`, which transforms the
output of `mapa-do-clima.html` in the `dutch-flow-tech-brazil` project — Regen
Studio's own analysis, produced inside the sales-agency relationship with Dutch
Flow Technology PTE LTD (Singapore) and reused here with their knowledge.

The composite "relevance" index present in the source was **removed** in this
version: it was built to answer a commercial question and does not belong on a
cultural-programme site.
